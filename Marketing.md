### How to Post on LinkedIn
 1. **Optimize Your Personal Profile First:** Ensure your LinkedIn headline reads like a value proposition (e.g., *"Founder @ [SaaS Name] — Helping B2B Agencies Recover Late Payments Automatically"*).
 2. **Post Content Types That Perform:**
   * **Text + Screenshots:** Write a short breakdown and attach a clean UI screenshot or workflow diagram.
   * **Short Video Demos:** Upload 30-to-60-second screen recordings showing how easy it is to set up a collection sequence.
 3. **Engagement Strategy:** Reply to every comment within the first hour of posting to boost feed distribution.
### Do You Need Business Verification?
 * **No, verification is not required to launch.** You can post, message leads, run cold outreach, and sign paying customers without a verified badge.
 * **How Verification Works:** Personal profiles can get verified by submitting a government ID via LinkedIn's identity partners (like Persona/CLEAR). Company Pages can request verification once basic criteria and administrator checks are completed.
 * **Recommendation:** Focus on acquiring your first 5–10 beta customers before prioritizing badge verifications.
### Building Professional Trust for a New SaaS
When handling sensitive financial workflows, potential clients need complete confidence in your security and reliability.
 * **Highlight Bring-Your-Own-Keys (BYOK) Architecture:** Emphasize that your SaaS does not hold or touch client money—payments go straight from the end client into the agency’s Stripe/PayPal account.
 * **Integrate via Official App Marketplaces:** Listing on the official Xero and QuickBooks marketplaces serves as a strong stamp of approval.
 * **Maintain Professional Documentation:** Create clear, accessible help guides, API documentation, and a dedicated security/privacy page detailing encryption standards.
 * **Provide Live Demos & White-Glove Onboarding:** Offer personal 1-on-1 walkthroughs and custom setup assistance for early customers to eliminate adoption friction.
